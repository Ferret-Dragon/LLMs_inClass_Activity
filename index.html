          How to use Financer

          Financer keeps track of what you spend and shows where it goes. Everything is saved in this browser on this device.

          Record spending

          1. On the Home page, fill in Add a transaction: a description, the amount, a category and the date.
          2. Press Add. The transaction appears at the top of Recent transactions, tinted with its category color.
          3. Picked the wrong category? Click the transaction (or its category label) and choose the right one from the list. Press Escape to cancel.
          4. To remove a transaction, press the ✕ at the end of its row.

          Review your transactions

          • Home shows your 5 most recent transactions. Press Show all to list every one.
          • Use the category chips above the list to filter. Select one or more categories, or All to clear the filter.

          See where your money goes

          • The Spending by category pie chart shows each category's share of your total.
          • Click a slice, its legend entry, or a category in the Categories box to open that category's page.

          Category pages

          • Each category has its own page, shaded in a muted tone of its color, with one card per purchase.
          • Press Edit label to change the category's name or color without leaving the page.
          • Use Add a card to record spending straight into that category.
          • Press ← All spending to return to Home.

          Manage categories

          1. In the Categories box on Home, press Edit categories.
          2. Add: type a name, pick a color and press Add category.
          3. Rename: edit the name in its box and press Enter.
          4. Recolor: click the color swatch and pick a new color.
          5. Delete: press Delete, then Confirm delete. Its transactions are kept and shown as Uncategorized.
          6. Press Done when you have finished.
